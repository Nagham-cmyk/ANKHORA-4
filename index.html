Treasures of Egypt

Discover the stories behind some of Egypt's most remarkable ancient artifacts.

01

Mask of Tutankhamun

The magnificent golden funerary mask of Pharaoh Tutankhamun is one of ancient Egypt's most iconic masterpieces. Crafted from gold and decorated with colorful inlays, it symbolized royal power and protection in the afterlife.

02

Ancient Egyptian Mummies

Mummification was an important ancient Egyptian practice designed to preserve the body for the afterlife. These remains reveal fascinating details about ancient beliefs, rituals, and daily life.

03

Ushabti Figurines

Ushabti were small funerary figurines placed in tombs to serve the deceased in the afterlife. These fascinating objects reflect ancient Egyptian beliefs about life beyond death.
